# Plugin: Recurring Billing

Subscriptions and recurring charges, for example monthly POS lease fees charged to merchants.

## Benefits of a plugin over building it into the core
- **Fills the deferred POS-invoicing gap:** without changing the core invoice model now.
- **Schedules and dunning per org:** each seller sets its own plans, retries and grace periods.
- **Posts only through the money interface:** charges apply exactly once and stay consistent with the three ledgers.
- **Reusable by any seller:** platform, manufacturers and suppliers alike.
