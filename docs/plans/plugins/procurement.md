# Plugin: Procurement

Purchase orders with approval workflows, budgets and supplier comparison for larger orgs.

## Benefits of a plugin over building it into the core
- **Grows with the org:** small merchants keep one-tap reorder, while bigger orgs turn on full procurement.
- **Approval rules per org:** the second-person rule and limits are configured, not hard-coded.
- **Works with Inventory:** received purchase orders post stock movements, and reorder rules can raise draft purchase orders.
- **Leaves the B2B core alone:** the order and invoice model stays simple.
