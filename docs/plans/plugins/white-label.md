# Plugin: White-label Branding

A manufacturer's subtree sees its logo, colours and wording.

## Benefits of a plugin over building it into the core
- **Inherited down the tree:** one `SUBTREE` grant brands the whole network.
- **Configuration, not code:** no per-partner builds.
- **Premium feature:** turned on only for partners who pay.
- **Contained:** branding mistakes affect only that partner's network.
