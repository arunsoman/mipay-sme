# Plugin: Loyalty

Points or stamp cards that merchants run for their customers, earned on confirmed payments.

## Benefits of a plugin over building it into the core
- **Optional per merchant:** only merchants that want loyalty pay for and see it.
- **Driven by events:** it listens for confirmed payments, and the payment flow is unchanged.
- **Network-wide programs:** a manufacturer can sponsor a loyalty program across its subtree.
- **Liabilities separated:** points are tracked apart from real balances.
