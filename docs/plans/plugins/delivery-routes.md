# Plugin: Delivery & Routes

Route planning for distributor vans, proof of delivery, and delivery confirmations that feed inventory shipment receipts.

## Benefits of a plugin over building it into the core
- **Only for orgs that deliver:** merchants and lenders never see it.
- **Driven by events:** it reacts to shipments and invoices, with no direct coupling to the B2B core.
- **Mapping and routing providers can change:** without affecting anything else.
- **Separate mobile workflow:** a lightweight driver view can be built against its own API.
